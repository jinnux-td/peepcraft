# @kit/peeps-creator

How to use it, set the default colour of each item, and set up the pieces of an item: [docs/peeps-colours.md](../../docs/peeps-colours.md).

**Assembly:** a character creator for [`@kit/peeps`](../peeps) as one React component. Drop it in a page or in a dashboard panel; `@kit/peeps` itself stays free of any UI, so other packages can use `Peep` alone.

```tsx
import { PeepCreator } from '@kit/peeps-creator'

<PeepCreator
  defaultState={{ type: 'sitting', bg: 'transparent' }}   // anything left out is the default
  onChange={(state) => save(state)}                       // the whole state, after every change
  onExport={({ kind, name, blob }) => upload(name, blob)} // without it, Save downloads the file
  theme={theme} onThemeChange={setTheme}                  // or defaultTheme="noir" and let it keep the theme
  showThemeButton title="Avatar" className="my-panel"
/>
```

- **Parts:** a tab per category (type, body, hair, face, facial hair, accessories), each an option grid of small pictures on the current figure.
- **Extra:** a tab after Accessories for what the peep wears beside it, the `extras` of [`@kit/things`](../things) (speech, round and thought bubbles by the head; an aura around the peep, following its outline; more go in the same group with a `place`). One at a time, or None. Its dots, **Extra lines** and **Extra fill**, start in the extra's own colours (a bubble `#111111` on `#f2f2f2`, the aura gold `#f4b400` on pale yellow `#fff3bf`) and start over when an extra is chosen again. The aura is drawn behind the peep, its outline grown 150 units out (an SVG filter), so it fits every type and pose. The stage grows to hold it, and **Add** puts it in the scene with the peep, where its colours stay editable.
- **Figures:** a last tab with the 38 whole illustrations of [`@kit/transhumans`](../transhumans) (and a tile to go back to building a peep). A figure replaces the peep on the stage; colours, scale, rotate, flip, background and both exports work on it, parts do not apply, and the dice of the tab picks another figure.
- **Scene:** the last tab composes a picture of many: **Add** copies the current peep or figure into the scene, and the stage shows the scene while the tab is open. The left panel holds what can be added; the right panel lists the members, as tall as the window allows, then scrolling. Drag a member on the stage to move it across or up and down (one undo step per drag), or pick it (its tile, or a click on the stage) to set its position, size and lift (height above the ground, from -20 % to 80 %: a mug on a table is 25 %), flip it (`F`), send it back or to the front (or drag its tile to another place in the list), or remove it. Everyone stands on one ground line (the feet come from the measured outline), on the scene's background. Each member keeps its own colours, editable when picked: Lines, Fill, and the named parts of a thing (the leaves and the pot of a plant, the cushions of a sofa), one palette for the colour chosen; a small handle at each corner of the picked member resizes it, keeping the opposite corner where it is. Under the stage, **Ratio** (1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 3:4, 9:16; members keep their place across the width) and **Zoom** (0.5 to 2, about the ground line) set the picture; the PNG follows the ratio (long side 1024 px). Save exports the whole scene (`scene.svg`, `scene.png`). **Things** adds one of the 22 drawings of [`@kit/things`](../things) in the current colours. **Uploads** takes a PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or SVG (5 MB at most) and keeps it in this browser (IndexedDB `kit-peeps-creator`), never on a server: it stays after a reload until its × is pressed, which also takes it out of the scene, or until the site's data is cleared. An upload is drawn as an `<image>` with a data URL, so an SVG's scripts never run and the exports carry the picture. Undo covers add, remove, flip and order. The scene is `state.scene`, so `defaultState` and `onChange` carry it. It is drawn with the `Scene` of `@kit/peeps`.
- **Dice:** one per tab changes that tab only (never to the same choice twice); **Shuffle all** changes every part. A transparent background is left alone by Shuffle.
- **Colours:** a peep starts with skin `#ffdbbe`, and every item comes in its own colours, which it carries (`atom.pieces`, picked once from its name by `scripts/peeps-item-colors.ts` when the skin areas are written, or set by hand in the labels), so its thumbnail shows what you get: hair and beards black, an outfit keeps to one theme, warm or cold, muted, and three colours at most: a piece drawn black stays black, a jacket takes an accent and trousers a dark, the top under them is white when they are dark and black when they are not (alone it takes the accent), and shoes repeat one of the others; the whites of a face (eyes, teeth, a tear) are white whatever the Fill. Each tab shows the colours of what it is about above its items, as named dots: Type has Skin, Lines and Fill; Hair has Hair (the hair only, its outline stays), Hat (a hat, a band, beads), Line (the head's black lines) and Line 1 (its white lines over black, such as the dashes of twists; Line 2 and on when a lasso names more) and Skin; Body has the pieces the outfit carries (Top, Jacket, Bottoms, Shoes, or any other a label names, such as a Tie), Clothes (every other white area) and Skin; Face one, Facial hair the beard's; Accessories its lines, and Frame for glasses with a white frame (glasses-4, white whatever the Fill); Figures Lines and Fill. A dot opens its swatches (Skin offers skin tones first, Hair hair colours, any colour under ⋯ and the wheel); a picked colour wins until another item is chosen in that slot, which comes in its own again, even the same item chosen again (lines and fill stay), and "Back to its own colour" clears one.
- **Thumbnails:** each item by itself, so choices are easy to tell apart; **On peep** in a part tab's header shows them on the whole peep instead (off on every load). The button beside it shows the items as a grid or a list (picture and full name a row); hovering an item shows its name. In Contrast the pictures sit on light grey `#f2f2f2` whatever the background. A tab opens at the top of its items; after a dice the grid (not the page) scrolls to the new choice.
- **Locks:** a padlock beside each part tab's dice and on Background keeps it when **Shuffle all** is pressed (`state.locked`); the dice of a tab still changes its part.
- **Contrast:** a button under Background draws everything in Lines and Fill only (black on white, as the pack does): no item colours, no skin, no picks; thumbnails, exports and scene copies follow, the tabs show no colour dots, and turning it off brings the colours back (`state.mono`).
- **Sliders:** a dot on the track marks the default (Scale 1, Rotate 0°, Zoom 1×, ...), and a drag that comes within 5 % of it (a step and a half at least) lands on it, so it is easy to go back to and takes a little more pull to leave.
- **Look:** background (a colour, or **transparent**); a click on a part of the peep on the stage opens its tab; scale, rotate, flip (also the `F` key), undo / redo / reset.
- **Numbers:** every slider has its value as a box to type in (kept in range) and a reset icon back to the default (scale 1, rotate 0°, position 50 %, size 1, lift 0 %, zoom 1×).
- **Export:** SVG, and PNG at 1×, 2×, 3× or 4× (1024 px on the long side at 1×; the size is shown before saving). Transparent stays transparent in both (no rectangle in the SVG, alpha in the PNG).
- **Themes:** `sunny`, `glass`, `noir`, `violet`, scoped to the component (`data-pc-theme`), so a host page is never restyled. The stylesheet ships inside the component.
- **Size:** the layout follows the width of the component (container queries), not the window: three columns when wide, stacked in a narrow panel or on a phone.

## Folders
```
src/state.ts, random.ts, scene.ts, export.ts, types.ts   plain TypeScript: choosing, history, dice, the picture, the exports (tested with node --test)
src/styles.ts                                            the stylesheet and the four themes
src/components/                                          PeepCreator (the whole), PartPicker, SceneSources and SceneMembers, Thumb, Stage, Swatches, Slider, ThemeMenu, icons
```
Each piece is exported, so a dashboard can use the whole creator or only, say, `PartPicker` with its own state.

Depends on `@kit/peeps`, `@kit/transhumans` and `@kit/things`; peer dependencies `react` and `react-dom` (>= 18). Node >= 22.23.4 for the tests; the components are `.tsx` and need a bundler (esbuild, Vite). `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` run inside the copy (copy `peeps`, `transhumans` and `things` with it). The browser test is `examples/peeps/e2e.test.ts`.
